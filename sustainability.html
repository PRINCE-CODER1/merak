Planet First. Always.

We believe in making clothes that last, not clothes that end up in landfills. Discover our commitment to sustainable fashion.

Ethical Manufacturing

Every garment we create is cut, sewn, and printed in India by skilled artisans who are paid fair wages and work in safe conditions. We maintain full transparency in our supply chain, ensuring that every hand that touches our product is treated with respect and dignity.

Sustainable Materials

We use 100% organic cotton and recycled polyester in our core collections. By choosing organic, we save thousands of liters of water per garment and eliminate the use of harmful pesticides that damage local ecosystems. Our heavy-duty fabrics mean your clothes last longer, reducing the need for constant replacements.

Zero-Waste Packaging

From our studio to your door, your order arrives in 100% compostable mailers. We've eliminated single-use plastics from our packaging and use recycled paper for all our tags and inserts.
